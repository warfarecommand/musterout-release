# Warfare Command email framework (09 Oct 2026)

Principle: every email earns its place. No empty emails, one action each, no tracking pixels, and the reader controls what they get.

## What people can sign up for (the preference page shows exactly these)

| Stream | What it is | Cadence | Default |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Muster Out corrections | A fix to the regulation pack or a release that changes what your board tells you, with the update message. | Only when it happens. Expect a few a year. | On for Muster Out signups |
| Warfare Command Monthly | One short email: what shipped, what is next, one AI move worth trying, and one question we want your answer to. Release notes and new lessons ride here. | First Tuesday of the month | On for everyone |
| Weekly MARADMIN roundup | The week's possible separation and retirement matches from MARADMIN Watch. | Mondays, only in weeks with at least one match | Off; one click to add |
| New lessons | A single note when AI 102, 103, or 104 opens. | Only at launch | On for AI 101 signups |

Unsubscribe from everything is one click in every email.

## Rules that keep it from being spam
- Caps: at most one non-correction email per person per week, and at most four per month. A correction is exempt and rare.
- No empty sends: the weekly roundup skips quiet weeks; the Monthly skips a month with nothing worth saying.
- No standalone feedback campaign: the Monthly carries one question; replies go to feedback@warfarecommand.com.
- No drip sequence: one welcome email after confirming, with the preference link and a button to add the weekly roundup. Nothing else is automated toward a new subscriber.
- No open or click tracking.

## Who sends what
- Automatic: confirmation, welcome, weekly roundup (opt-in, built from the Watch feed).
- Drafted by Claude, sent with the owner's one-word OK: the Monthly (from the changelog, shipped tools, suggestions received, and the Watch stats), corrections, and lesson launches.

## Templates
confirm-subscription, welcome, correction-alert, monthly (replaces monthly-update; release notes and the feedback question fold in), weekly-roundup, lesson-launch (replaces release-notes as a standalone), unsubscribed page only (no email after someone leaves).
