# Call brief for TAP or VSO

Prepare for a call with your TAP counselor or accredited VSO in your [Project](../PROJECT.md).

## Add these files

- Your board, read directly with artifact tools, your completion evidence or VA notices, and the issue for your call.
- Add your own records as they are to your private Project.

## Paste this

```text
My own records can stay as they are in my private Project, including files with identifiers. Never repeat SSNs, DoD IDs, VA claim numbers, account numbers or similar identifiers in replies, or store or write them to my board or your notes. Strip them and other people's personal details from drafts for others, including call briefs, spouse briefs and advisor notes. Never ask for an SSN, password or CAC PIN.

Read my Muster Out board with your artifact tools before you begin. If you cannot read it, say so and ask for the board link.

Help me prepare a call brief for my TAP counselor or accredited VSO. State my purpose, known facts, missing facts, and questions in priority order. Cite the file and page for anything you use, plus board item titles. Say unknown instead of guessing diagnosis dates, deadlines, or contact details. Label the result a draft. Give me a short opening statement and evidence list. Do not make a call or change anything.
```

## You get

You get an opening statement, evidence list, and questions.

## Check it with

You check facts and cited pages; your TAP counselor confirms transition evidence, and an accredited VSO confirms claim procedures and whether they apply.
