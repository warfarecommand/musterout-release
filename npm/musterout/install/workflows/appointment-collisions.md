# Appointment conflicts

Find appointments that overlap in your [Project](../PROJECT.md).

## Add these files

- Appointment confirmations, approved leave or SkillBridge dates, and Your Muster Out board, read directly with artifact tools.
- Optional: Calendar access to read appointments.
- Add your own records as they are to your private Project.

## Paste this

```text
My own records can stay as they are in my private Project, including files with identifiers. Never repeat SSNs, DoD IDs, VA claim numbers, account numbers or similar identifiers in replies, or store or write them to my board or your notes. Strip them and other people's personal details from drafts for others, including call briefs, spouse briefs and advisor notes. Never ask for an SSN, password or CAC PIN.

Read my Muster Out board with your artifact tools before you begin. If you cannot read it, say so and ask for the board link.

Compare my appointments with confirmed absences, exams, and SkillBridge dates. Show overlaps and possible conflicts in a table. Cite the file and page for anything you use. Say unknown instead of guessing times or time zones. Separate deadlines, planning targets, and confirmed events. Give me questions to resolve conflicts. Label the result a draft. Do not move, cancel, create, or send anything.
```

## You get

You get a conflict table and scheduling questions.

## Check it with

Each appointment office confirms times and time zones; your command confirms leave and SkillBridge changes before you request a calendar change.
