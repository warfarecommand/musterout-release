# Income after separation

See how long your money covers expenses in your [Project](../PROJECT.md).

## Add these files

- LES, confirmed income and expenses, offer letters, and known payment dates.
- Optional: savings you choose to include.
- Black out your Social Security number and account numbers.

## Paste this

```text
Read my Muster Out board with your artifact tools before you begin. If you cannot read it, say so and ask for the board link.

Build a monthly budget worksheet from my confirmed figures. Separate income, recurring expenses, costs paid once, and assumptions. Show the arithmetic and months with a gap. Cite the file and page for anything you use. Say unknown instead of guessing pay or benefits dates. Compare scenarios with labeled assumptions. Verify changing rates from official sources. Label the result a draft. For a VA scenario, use only the person's tier choices after they review official criteria, the existing combined-rating calculator and the official pay table with its effective date. Label it "Estimate. VA decides." Never choose a tier, add symptoms or promise a payment.
```

## You get

You get a monthly worksheet, assumptions, and figures to confirm.

## Check it with

IPAC or the pay office confirms pay and leave amounts; the issuing agency confirms benefits, and a qualified financial counselor reviews the budget.
