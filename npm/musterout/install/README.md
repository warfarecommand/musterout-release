# Muster Out

A personal separation and retirement planner for Marines. It shows what is due next for your separation and the regulation text each task cites.

Your board lives in your own Claude account, and Anthropic hosts it under your login. The maintainers cannot read it. Registration, reports and email are separate choices, each described in PRIVACY.md. Each cited item carries its quoted source text. Every citation has a local source copy in the pack, and a script checks each quote against it; those copies are snapshots, so open the official source for current wording. The board also models your leave, keeps a claim tab for the VA timeline and evidence, and answers questions from your board through an Ask box.

The page shows three kinds of date and labels each one:
- A **rule deadline** is a date or window the cited text requires. It may depend on your separation date or on another event.
- A **planning target** is a date selected for planning, and the board marks it as a target. Any window a regulation sets still governs it.
- A **confirmed event** is a date you set after an approval or a booking.

Pilot. Marine Corps only for now. Paths covered: resigning a regular commission, retiring, separating at EAS, active-duty Marine IDES and current TDRL follow-up; officer and enlisted; Reserve affiliation and SkillBridge as options. Enlisted retirement covers FMCR transfer with 20 to 29 years of active service and direct retirement with 30 or more. The reporting unit submits to MMSR-2 through unit diary; IPAC confirms eligibility and dates. `SUPPORT.md` lists covered paths and the offices for paths outside the pack.

Claude authenticates the publisher signature and exact board and guide bytes with the independently pinned verifier. If the chat cannot run the security check, setup stops before publishing. Existing boards keep working. It asks you to tap Publish for your private board, then gives you the board link. Open it and answer a few questions there: path, date, and grade group. Every other answer is optional, and the board builds your plan from them. Setup takes about 5 minutes in a Claude chat on a phone or computer. If the phone app cannot open the board, use claude.ai in your phone browser.

The 20 minute lesson is optional background for anyone new to AI. Take it before or after setup.

## Developer folder installs
Developers can clone [the public release repository](https://github.com/warfarecommand/musterout-release). Folder updates authenticate the signed install manifest and exact-version package, keep `my/`, and republish at the existing board address. Everyone can use the setup message above in Claude Code or the desktop Code tab.

## What it costs and what it sends
Muster Out costs nothing beyond your Claude plan. Your plan opens first. Then the board asks whether you agree to register it so the maintainer can count boards. Saying no leaves the plan fully usable. If you agree, the board opens a registration page that sends a random board key, the version, your path, your grade group, a campaign code if you used one, and the setup day. These counts are pseudonymous: they count boards, not people, and they carry no plan text or medical records. Optional usage counts are off in this version. Boards set up before this version keep their original count agreement. `PRIVACY.md` lists every field, who can see it in transit, and how deletion works.

## How to delete
Delete the artifact from its claude.ai page menu. If you cloned a folder install, delete the Muster Out folder too. That removes the board and its database from your account. Three things remain: your own chat history with Claude, which you control in your Claude settings; anything you copied or exported; and the pseudonymous count or lesson result, if you sent one. `PRIVACY.md` says how to have the count or a lesson result deleted.

## Not advice
Muster Out cites regulations and tracks dates. It is not legal, financial, or medical advice, and it does not predict VA ratings. IPAC, your TAP counselor, your VSO, and your command decide what applies to you; confirm with them before you act.
