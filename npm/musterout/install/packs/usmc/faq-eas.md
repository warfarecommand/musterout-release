# Ordinary end of active service

Start with the next step for your path. Ask the office named in each answer to confirm your individual dates and requirements; source references are included for review.

## 1. EAS separation requests

For ordinary enlisted expiration of enlistment, MCO 1900.16 CH-3, 1005.1, authorizes commanders to discharge Marines when the enlistment or extension expires. That paragraph does not prescribe the retirement or resignation application window for an ordinary EAS checkout. Ask your personnel office to confirm the local checkout submission deadline and whether your contract or officer career designation requires a different separation action. Ask IPAC to confirm the separation authority, date, and local processing requirements for your record. Confirm with: IPAC and commanding officer.

## 2. Release authority and separation location

Commanders are authorized to discharge enlisted Marines at expiration of enlistment under MCO 1900.16 CH-3, 1005.1. Paragraph 1006.1 directs commanders to separate Marines when due or directed, subject to exceptions for location, directed transfers, and unauthorized absence. Overseas separation processing has additional location and transfer rules in paragraph 1006.5. Confirm your final checkout day and release authority with your personnel office; a displayed EAS date is not approval to leave. Confirm with: IPAC and commanding officer.

## 3. Remaining Reserve service

MCO 1900.16 CH-3, 1005.3, provides for transfer of enlisted Marines with an uncompleted military service obligation to the IRR unless reenlistment or another discharge direction applies. For officers, MARADMIN 045/25, paragraph 3(a)(1), describes the initial 8-year obligation and checking the EOS date in the Basic Individual Record. DAP is a separate opportunity to affiliate with the SMCR or IMA, rather than proof that every Marine leaving active duty owes drilling service. See MARADMIN 454/26, paragraphs 2(a) and 6(a), and have the office distinguish remaining obligation from a voluntary billet choice. Confirm with: IPAC and Career Counselor.

## 4. Terminal leave

Terminal leave requires CO approval and completion of all administrative and medical separation requirements. MCO 1900.16 CH-3, 1010.2, says it runs continuously to EAS or Retired List/FMCR transfer. Paragraph 1010.2.d says to submit the separation request "at least 120 days plus the amount of desired leave (to include PTAD)" before the effective date and to enter the planned detachment date on a diary request. Paragraph 1010.2.e requires desired leave dates and duration with the separation request. Have IPAC and MMSR-2 compare that lead time with any applicable message/manual request windows. Complete STR delivery and all separation gates before departure on terminal leave. The board's 90-day item is a final confirmation target. Confirm with: CO, IPAC and HQMC MMSR-2.

## 5. House and job hunting PTAD eligibility

Ordinary expiration of active service is not itself listed as an eligible reason for transition PTAD in MCO 1050.3J, chapter 5, 1.c(11)(a). The listed circumstances include specified involuntary separations, approved retirement, and the named incentive programs. The 20-day CONUS rule applies to Marines who qualify under that list, rather than every Marine separating. See MCO 1050.3J, chapter 5, 1.c(11)(a-b); ask your commanding officer whether an exception applies to your circumstances. Confirm with: commanding officer and IPAC.

## 6. Combining SkillBridge, PTAD and terminal leave

MARADMIN 280/24, 3.a-c, sets 120/90-day category limits and rank-based approval, and 625/24, 5.d, repeats the limits, while NAVMC 1700.2B, 4.a(1) and enclosure (2), 2, uses CO/OIC approval, a 180-day boundary and no added rank/grade restrictions. Have your SkillBridge coordinator and approving command confirm the governing authority and duration before using dates. MARADMIN 280/24 requires PTAD before leave within its combined absence limit. Get written confirmation of the governing version and approval route. Confirm the combined absence cap and sequence before using dates. Confirm with: installation SkillBridge coordinator and approving command.

## 7. Family TRICARE coverage

Ask TRICARE to confirm each family member's eligibility, enrollment deadline, costs and coverage after active duty. MARADMIN 454/26, 3.a, states that transitioning Active Component Marines who affiliate with the Selected Reserve without a break in service are eligible for 180 days of premium-free TAMP and that eligible Selected Reserve Marines may enroll in TRICARE Reserve Select afterward. Confirm each dependent's enrollment requirements and whether the Continued Health Care Benefit Program (CHCBP) applies. Have the benefits office confirm your qualifying status and each family member's coverage dates. Confirm with: TRICARE benefits office and DEERS office.

## 8. BDD and the separation physical

The VA guide v6.1, Module 3, p. 72, says to "Submit your BDD claims between 180 and 90 days" before the final active-duty day. It requires a known separation date, completed SHA Part A, records for the entire service history and availability for exams for 45 days after filing. The final active-duty day is the filing anchor. MCO 1900.16 CH-3, 1011.2, says to schedule SHPE at least 6 months before separation. Guide p. 74 says a completed SHA returned to DoD at least 30 days before separation can satisfy the discharge examination requirement. A scheduled VA exam alone does not satisfy that condition. Confirm with: VA benefits office or accredited VSO and military medical clinic.

## 9. Transition counseling and training

MCO 1700.31A, enclosure (2), chapter 5, paragraph 3.a-e, requires IC and pre-separation counseling no later than 365 days before EAS, TRS workshops at 180 days, Capstone at 120 days and Commander's Verification at 90 days. Chapter 8, paragraph 4.a, allows retiree IC to begin as soon as possible during the 24-month period preceding anticipated retirement; paragraph 4.b allows other Marines to complete IC 18 months before separation or release from active duty. Count days from the confirmed EAS, separate from a Retired List action date. Keep actual appointments as confirmed events. Chapter 8, paragraph 2; chapter 9, paragraph 2; chapter 10, paragraph 3; and chapter 12, paragraph 1.c, require the sequence IC, pre-separation counseling, TRS, Capstone and Commander's Verification. Complete ROOB as required IC prework under Appendix A. NAVMC 1700.2B, enclosure (2), paragraph 7, requires all transition requirements complete before SkillBridge participation. Confirm with: installation TRP office, UTC, IPAC, CO and SkillBridge coordinator.

## 10. Records before final checkout

Review your DD-214 information with IPAC using MCO 1900.16 CH-3, Appendix B, which describes preparation of the separation record. Submit your complete Service Treatment Record before transition PTAD, terminal leave, transfer to the IRR, separation, or retirement as required by paragraph 1101.7.b. An officer accepting a Reserve commission also needs the updated NAVMC 763 and accurate storage of separation records in OMPF under MARADMIN 045/25, 5.b. Have IPAC identify the current DD-214 form edition and required local checkout documents. Confirm with: IPAC and military medical records office.

## Final household goods move

Contact the installation DMO/TMO for final household goods (HHG) move counseling. The board sets a planning appointment target 90 days before its separation or retirement date. JTR, 1 OCT 2026, paragraph 052012 (A.1 and C) authorizes ordinary separation shipment to home of record or place last entered active duty, within the PCS weight allowance. Authority ends on the 181st day unless a written request reaches the transportation officer before the end of the 180th day. Timely applications require turnover as soon as practicable. Paragraph 052013 (A.1.b and B) gives qualifying retirement and FMCR home-of-selection authority with a 3-year turnover period for active-duty termination dates on or after 24 JUN 2022. Table 5-52, item 1, ends non-temporary storage 1 year from active-duty termination. Exceptions and extensions require review. Have DMO/TMO confirm the applicable category, entitlement, destination, weight, exact shipment limit and separate storage expiration against your orders. Record the appointment and office-confirmed dates separately. Confirm with: installation DMO/TMO.

## If BDD is missed

BDD is the preferred route; intent to file protects the potential effective date when BDD is missed. The VA intent to file page, under the intent definition and claim-completion time limit, says that an intent sets a potential effective date and gives you 1 year to complete and file the claim. Record the actual VA intent date and keep the receipt. Have VA or your VSO confirm its calendar-year anniversary, then ask Claude to record that fixed rule deadline on the separate completion item. It is counted from the intent date. Confirm with: VA benefits office or accredited VSO.

## VA dental and family CHAMPVA

VA dental care eligibility, Class IV rating and unemployability statements, permits any needed dental care for a qualifying 100 percent service-connected rating or individual unemployability paid at the 100 percent rate. Permanence is not required; temporary 100 percent ratings do not qualify for Class IV. VA CHAMPVA benefits, "Eligibility for CHAMPVA", permits qualifying spouses and dependent children of veterans rated permanent and total for a service-connected disability. Anyone eligible for TRICARE is excluded, including retiree families eligible for TRICARE. Confirm with: VA dental clinic eligibility office, VA CHAMPVA eligibility office and DEERS/TRICARE benefits office.
