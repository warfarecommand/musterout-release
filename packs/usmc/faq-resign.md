# Resigning a Regular officer commission

Answers use the 8 copied regulation texts and 3 supplemental source texts. External seed citations do not establish an answer here. Paragraphs refer to the printed documents, including conflicts and older instructions that require office confirmation.

## 1. When must I submit a resignation request?

MARADMIN 175/24, 2, directs submission not more than 18 months and not less than 6 months before the requested resignation date. MCO 1900.16 CH-3, 5003.2, still prints a 4-to-14-month unit diary window and says MCTFS will reject diary requests outside it. The pack does not settle which window governs diary acceptance or whether a local routing date satisfies submission. IPAC and MMSR-2 must decide before submission. MARADMIN 175/24, 3, also permits the Director, Manpower Management, to approve a different date based on service needs and other constraints. Confirm with: IPAC and HQMC MMSR-2.

## 2. How do I submit, and do I need a letter?

Your reporting senior must counsel you before submission, and the page 11 entry must record your Reserve commission choice. The unit submits the resignation by unit diary and reports the transaction code for that choice. A Figure 5-1 letter through the chain of command is required when you need a paragraph 5002 waiver or request a Reserve commission without the commanding officer's recommendation. See MCO 1900.16 CH-3, 5003.1, 5003.2, and 5003.4. Confirm with: reporting senior and IPAC.

## 3. Who approves, and how do I know the request is approved?

The Secretary of the Navy has delegated resignation approval authority to CMC (DC M&RA), and approval can be deferred or denied to meet service needs. Receipt or a pending flag is not the same as approval. The unit receives notification through the electronic diary feedback report, and release authority is issued through MCTFS unit diary processing. See MCO 1900.16 CH-3, 5002.1-2, 5003.3, and 5005.1. Confirm with: IPAC and HQMC MMSR-2.

## 4. Can I change or withdraw my request, and can PCS orders affect it?

The manual states a general restriction after PCS issuance or notification, but permits an otherwise eligible officer to request cancellation with resignation under 5002.3.a(1)-(4). The effective resignation date must be no later than the last day of the estimated arrival month. Route a written request with command endorsements to MMSR-2. Submit at least 120 days before estimated arrival and no later than 10 working days after receipt of orders. Paragraph (4) addresses notification less than 120 days before arrival and the effective date. For a withdrawal or modification, 5004.2 prints "before 45 days from the effective date of the resignation or commencement of separation leave." Have IPAC and MMSR-2 confirm both anchors and the governing deadline. The board's conservative target is 46 calendar days before the earlier anchor, moved back for a weekend; it stays unresolved until actual leave start or no leave is confirmed. Confirm with: officer assignments monitor, IPAC and HQMC MMSR-2.

## 5. Can I take terminal leave?

Terminal leave requires CO approval and completion of all administrative and medical separation requirements. MCO 1900.16 CH-3, 1010.2, says it runs continuously to EAS or Retired List/FMCR transfer. Paragraph 1010.2.d says to submit the separation request "at least 120 days plus the amount of desired leave (to include PTAD)" before the effective date and to enter the planned detachment date on a diary request. Paragraph 1010.2.e requires desired leave dates and duration with the separation request. Have IPAC and MMSR-2 compare that lead time with any applicable message/manual request windows. Complete STR delivery and all separation gates before departure on terminal leave. The board's 90-day item is a final confirmation target. Confirm with: CO, IPAC and HQMC MMSR-2.

## 6. Can I combine SkillBridge, PTAD, and terminal leave?

MARADMIN 280/24, 3.a-c, sets 120/90-day category limits and rank-based approval, and 625/24, 5.d, repeats the limits, while NAVMC 1700.2B, 4.a(1) and enclosure (2), 2, uses CO/OIC approval, a 180-day boundary and no added rank/grade restrictions. Authority and duration remain unresolved. MARADMIN 280/24 requires PTAD before leave within its combined absence limit. Get written confirmation of the governing version and approval route. Confirm the combined absence cap and sequence before using dates. Confirm with: installation SkillBridge coordinator and approving command.

## 7. How do I obtain a Reserve commission and use DAP?

MARADMIN 045/25, paragraphs 2 and 3(b)(1), requires at least 4 months from HQMC receipt of the Reserve appointment request for approval and says the career-designated officer's processing follows resignation approval and entry of EAS in the record. Under MARADMIN 454/26, 5.b, an officer's DAP request window is 180 to 60 calendar days before EAS, and approval of a Reserve commission before EAS is required for DAP eligibility. Reserve commission approval appears in the planned separation code, and the updated NAVMC 763 must be issued before separation, signed by the specified higher-grade administering officer, returned, and uploaded to OMPF. See MARADMIN 045/25, 4.a and 5.b; record and confirm the HQMC receipt date with RAM-1. The commission process and DAP request are separate actions; a 7-month resignation target does not promise Reserve approval by EAS. Confirm with: IPAC, RAM-1, and Career Counselor.

## 8. What happens to my family's TRICARE?

The pack does not settle this for your family's eligibility, enrollment deadlines, costs, or coverage after a resignation or ordinary EAS. MARADMIN 454/26, 3.a, states that transitioning Active Component Marines who affiliate with the Selected Reserve without a break in service are eligible for 180 days of premium-free TAMP and that eligible Selected Reserve Marines may enroll in TRICARE Reserve Select afterward. That paragraph does not establish every dependent's enrollment requirements or eligibility for CHCBP. Have the benefits office confirm your qualifying status and each family member's coverage dates. Confirm with: TRICARE benefits office and DEERS office.

## 9. When does BDD open, and when should I arrange my separation physical?

The copied VA guide v6.1, Module 3, p. 72, says to "Submit your BDD claims between 180 and 90 days" before the final active-duty day. It requires a known separation date, completed SHA Part A, records for the entire service history and availability for exams for 45 days after filing. The final active-duty day is the filing anchor. MCO 1900.16 CH-3, 1011.2, says to schedule SHPE at least 6 months before separation. Guide p. 74 says a completed SHA returned to DoD at least 30 days before separation can satisfy the discharge examination requirement. A scheduled VA exam alone does not satisfy that condition. Confirm with: VA benefits office or accredited VSO and military medical clinic.

## 10. When should I complete transition counseling and training?

The supplied Hawaii TRP checklist sets IC and pre-separation counseling at 365 days before EAS, TRS at 180 days, Capstone at 120 days and Commander's Verification at 90 days, while MCO 1900.16 CH-3, 1101.1.b(1) and 1101.1.c(1), prints interview/training windows within 12 months of EAS or 24 months of retirement and no later than 90 days before separation or retirement. The checklist is installation guidance. Step Two says, "You must complete your IC before attending the Pre-Separation Counseling Brief." Step Three requires both before TRS registration. Complete ROOB as IC prework. NAVMC 1700.2B, enclosure (2), 3.c, requires completed TRS 180 days before separation; paragraph 7 requires all transition gates complete before S-PTAD. Confirm the applicable deadlines and earlier appointments before departure. Confirm with: installation TRP office, UTC and SkillBridge coordinator.
