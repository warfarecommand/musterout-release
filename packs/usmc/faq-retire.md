# Retirement and FMCR transfer

Answers use the 8 copied regulation texts and 3 supplemental source texts. External seed citations do not establish an answer here. Paragraphs refer to the printed documents, including conflicts and older instructions that require office confirmation.

## 1. When must I request retirement or transfer to the FMCR?

MARADMIN 175/24, 2, sets a retirement or resignation submission window of 18 to 6 months before the requested date for the Total Force. The manual still prints a 14-to-4-month diary window for officer retirement in 2004.1 and enlisted retirement or FMCR transfer in 7005.1. The pack does not settle how the interim message's retirement window is implemented in MCTFS or applied to an FMCR transfer request, so confirm both routing and receipt dates. Under glossary 252, retirement is on the 1st day of a month, except disability and Reserve retirement with pay at age 60, and FMCR transfer on the last day of a month (MCO 1900.16 CH-3, glossary 252 and 7005.1). Confirm with: IPAC and HQMC MMSR-2.

## 2. Am I eligible to retire, and is FMCR transfer the same action?

For commissioned officers, the manual describes 20 years and 1 day of active service, including 10 years of active commissioned service, and additional service-in-grade and obligation rules. Enlisted retirement and transfer to the FMCR are addressed separately in chapter 7, so do not apply the commissioned officer criteria to an enlisted request. Eligibility also depends on the specific officer category, service record, and any requested waiver. See MCO 1900.16 CH-3, 2002.2, 2003, 7003, and 7004; the pack does not settle your individual eligibility without a record review. Confirm with: IPAC and HQMC MMSR-2.

## 3. Who handles the request, and may I rely on my requested date?

Routine voluntary officer retirement requests go through MCTFS unit diary processing, and the officer signs Appendix J for the electronic service record. Reporting units also submit enlisted retirement or FMCR transfer requests through the unit diary and obtain the Marine's signed Appendix J. Mere submission is not approval, and the Director, Manpower Management, may approve a different retirement date. See MCO 1900.16 CH-3, 2004.1-2 and 7005.1-2, and MARADMIN 175/24, 3; the pack does not establish one final approving official for every retirement category and waiver. Confirm with: IPAC and HQMC MMSR-2.

## 4. Can I take terminal leave?

Terminal leave requires CO approval and completion of all administrative and medical separation requirements. MCO 1900.16 CH-3, 1010.2, says it runs continuously to EAS or Retired List/FMCR transfer. Paragraph 1010.2.d says to submit the separation request "at least 120 days plus the amount of desired leave (to include PTAD)" before the effective date and to enter the planned detachment date on a diary request. Paragraph 1010.2.e requires desired leave dates and duration with the separation request. Have IPAC and MMSR-2 compare that lead time with any applicable message/manual request windows. Complete STR delivery and all separation gates before departure on terminal leave. The board's 90-day item is a final confirmation target. Confirm with: CO, IPAC and HQMC MMSR-2.

## 5. Can I get transition PTAD for house or job hunting?

Officers and enlisted Marines with an approved retirement date are eligible for transition PTAD under MCO 1050.3J, chapter 5, 1.c(11)(a)5. The order provides up to 20 days for qualifying CONUS-based Marines and up to 30 consecutive days for qualifying OCONUS-based Marines, with specified domicile exceptions. Approval may be denied in whole or part if it interferes with mission accomplishment, and the permitted activities include house hunting, job hunting, and relocation. See MCO 1050.3J, chapter 5, 1.c(11)(b-e), and confirm any combined SkillBridge schedule against MARADMIN 280/24, 3.b. Confirm with: commanding officer and IPAC.

## 6. Can I combine SkillBridge, PTAD, and terminal leave?

MARADMIN 280/24, 3.a-c, sets 120/90-day category limits and rank-based approval, and 625/24, 5.d, repeats the limits, while NAVMC 1700.2B, 4.a(1) and enclosure (2), 2, uses CO/OIC approval, a 180-day boundary and no added rank/grade restrictions. Authority and duration remain unresolved. MARADMIN 280/24 requires PTAD before leave within its combined absence limit. Get written confirmation of the governing version and approval route. Confirm the combined absence cap and sequence before using dates. Confirm with: installation SkillBridge coordinator and approving command.

## 7. What happens to my family's TRICARE when I retire?

The copied pack does not establish current retiree or FMCR TRICARE enrollment deadlines, recovery options or individual coverage. MARADMIN 454/26, 5.e, makes retiring Marines, including medical retirees, ineligible for DAP. Its Selected Reserve TAMP statement in paragraph 3.a therefore does not establish a retiree's family coverage. MCO 1900.16 CH-3, 1407.1-5, requires accurate family information in DEERS and refers retired/FMCR medical benefits to TRICARE. Obtain enrollment rules, dependent coverage dates and ID card instructions from those offices. Confirm with: TRICARE benefits office and DEERS office.

## 8. When does BDD open, and when should I arrange my separation physical?

The copied VA guide v6.1, Module 3, p. 72, says to "Submit your BDD claims between 180 and 90 days" before the final active-duty day. It requires a known separation date, completed SHA Part A, records for the entire service history and availability for exams for 45 days after filing. The final active-duty day is the filing anchor. MCO 1900.16 CH-3, 1011.2, says to schedule SHPE at least 6 months before separation. Guide p. 74 says a completed SHA returned to DoD at least 30 days before separation can satisfy the discharge examination requirement. A scheduled VA exam alone does not satisfy that condition. Confirm with: VA benefits office or accredited VSO and military medical clinic.

## 9. When do I send DD 2656, and what if I decline full SBP coverage?

MCO 1900.16 CH-3, 1406.1, says the completed DD Form 2656 with the Privacy Act form must be faxed and mailed to DFAS at least 30 days before retirement or transfer to FMCR. Paragraph 1406.5 says elections other than full coverage require spousal concurrence and notarization. Paragraph 1406.6 says DFAS automatically deducts full SBP coverage if no election is made before the effective retirement date. Confirm the submission method and receipt with DFAS because this pack reproduces the manual's instructions rather than a current DFAS form workflow. Confirm with: retirement services office and DFAS retired pay office.

## 10. When should I complete transition counseling and training?

The supplied Hawaii TRP checklist sets IC and pre-separation counseling at 365 days before EAS, TRS at 180 days, Capstone at 120 days and Commander's Verification at 90 days, while MCO 1900.16 CH-3, 1101.1.b(1) and 1101.1.c(1), prints interview/training windows within 12 months of EAS or 24 months of retirement and no later than 90 days before separation or retirement. The checklist is installation guidance. Step Two says, "You must complete your IC before attending the Pre-Separation Counseling Brief." Step Three requires both before TRS registration. Complete ROOB as IC prework. NAVMC 1700.2B, enclosure (2), 3.c, requires completed TRS 180 days before separation; paragraph 7 requires all transition gates complete before S-PTAD. Confirm the applicable deadlines and earlier appointments before departure. Confirm with: installation TRP office, UTC and SkillBridge coordinator.
