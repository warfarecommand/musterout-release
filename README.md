# Muster Out

A free separation and retirement planner for Marines, with a personal board and an optional coach in your own Claude Project. Track active-duty separation, resignation, retirement, IDES and current TDRL follow-up. Prepare your own VA claim with records review, a personal statement helper and rating estimates using tiers you choose. You review, sign and file.

[Open Muster Out](https://musterout.warfarecommand.com/) | [Coverage](https://musterout.warfarecommand.com/support/) | [Coach guide](https://musterout.warfarecommand.com/coach/) | [Release notes](CHANGELOG.md)
