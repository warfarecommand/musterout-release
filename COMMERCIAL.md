# Organizational and Government Licensing

Warfare Command, offers paid written organizational licenses for Muster Out, Leader's Notebook, MARADMIN Watch, FITREP Desk, and covered warfarecommand.com site content.

## Who Needs a License

Organizations need a paid written license for copies or other acts requiring Licensor's copyright permission. This includes copies for deployment, hosting, distribution, required installation, integration, rebranding, resale, or client-service delivery. It excludes conduct authorized by an independent license, applicable law, or the completed-output permission in LICENSE. Organizations include governments, agencies, armed services, commands, units, the Marine Corps, MCCS and other instrumentalities, contractors, nonprofits, schools, and businesses. Personal accounts do not avoid this requirement.

Free Individual Use permits independent choice for your own personal or professional work on your own devices or accounts. A leader may prepare work for Marines they personally lead, including required documents, and submit permitted completed outputs. Required installation, organizational deployment, shared installation, and funded setup are excluded. See LICENSE for controlling definitions and exceptions. Earlier MIT, GitHub, and statutory rights remain unaffected.

A contractor employee's copies used to perform a customer contract require the contractor's separate written license, even with independent installation on a personal account and salary-only compensation. Personal or career use may qualify for Individual Use. Independent rights and express exceptions still apply.

## How to Ask

Email feedback@warfarecommand.com with the buyer's legal name, products, users, systems, deployment plan, and integration or distribution needs. Government buyers should identify the agency or unit and authorized contracting contact. A request grants no license or free trial.

## Government Buyers

Commercial software and documentation terms are available consistent with [FAR 12.212](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/12.212) and [DFARS 227.7202-3](https://www.acquisition.gov/dfars/227.7202-3-rights-commercial-computer-software-or-commercial-computer-software-documentation.), as applicable. Government rights belong in the contract license or addendum. Additional rights must be negotiated.

Federal terms must comply with federal law, without government indemnity or reliance on an employee's personal acknowledgment to bind the agency. Confirm procurement authority and eligibility before award. Address custom development and governmentwide sharing rights separately.

An ethics opinion does not itself authorize an award prohibited by [FAR 3.601](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/3.601). If [FAR 3.602](https://www.acquisition.gov/far/3.602) applies, the agency head or a designee not below the head of the contracting activity must authorize the exception based on a most compelling reason.

This page describes the process and grants no commercial or government license.
