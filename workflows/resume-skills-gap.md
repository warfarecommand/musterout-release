# Resume and skills gap

Match your experience to a job in your [Project](../PROJECT.md).

## Add these files

- Your resume, your training and experience records, and the actual job posting.
- Black out your Social Security number and other personal identifiers; leave other people's records out.

## Paste this

```text
Compare my documented experience with this job posting. Draft resume bullets and a table showing job requirements, evidence, missing experience, and questions for me. Cite the file and page for anything you use. Say unknown instead of guessing credentials, achievements, dates, or performance numbers. Label the result a draft. Help me identify claims I need to check against my records. Do not submit an application or change anything.
```

## You get

You get resume edits and a table of requirements, evidence, and gaps.

## Check it with

You verify every claim against your records; a career advisor or Hire Heroes USA coach reviews the target role and wording.
